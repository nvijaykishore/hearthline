What a plan contains

Three kinds of advice, each with the change it makes: a lower-emission routine, an indoor air step, and a sustainable swap. The numbers are calculated before any model writes a sentence.

  • Routine. When to be outside, how to do the school run, and when a mask is only the backup.
  • Indoor. Cooking, heating, the bedroom air, and when to open a window.
  • Alternative. The commute and the bag that does not get burned.

Hearthline is a planning aid. It is not a medical device and it is not an official air-quality bulletin.
